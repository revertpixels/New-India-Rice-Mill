THE NEW INDIA RICE MILLS PVT. LTD. — MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract this ZIP to a folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Use the navigation to visit each page.

PAGES INCLUDED
- index.html — long-form homepage with product range, buyer advantages, an enquiry flow, gallery previews and FAQs.
- about.html — company profile, business focus, product overview and location.
- products.html — product catalogue with filter control and enquiry preparation.
- products/swarna-rice.html — dedicated Swarna Rice page.
- products/ratna-rice.html — dedicated Ratna Rice page.
- products/bashkathi-rice.html — dedicated Bashkathi Rice page.
- products/minikit-rice.html — dedicated Minikit Rice page.
- process.html — general rice-industry process overview. It is not a claim about the mill’s exact equipment or process.
- gallery.html — multi-image gallery of real third-party rice and rice-industry photographs.
- contact.html — contact details, hours, access information, map and enquiry form.

DESIGN & FUNCTIONALITY
- Responsive forest-green / warm ivory / brass-gold visual system.
- Long-form layouts with section transitions, image compositions and editorial typography.
- Shared assets/style.css and assets/site.js.
- Mobile navigation, product filter, FAQ accordion and email enquiry actions.
- No prices, production capacity, certifications, export claims, testimonials or company history invented.

IMAGES
Actual third-party image URLs collected from public web image search are used for rice varieties, paddy fields, rice machinery and packaging imagery. The image files are not embedded inside the ZIP; internet access is required to load them. IMAGE_SOURCES.txt lists the sources and URLs. The photographs are illustrative, not verified pictures of the mill or its stock. Please check commercial rights and replace with original business-owned images before launching publicly.
