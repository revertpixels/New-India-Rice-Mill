The wider process

Every grain moves through a chain of careful steps.

Rice processing is more than a single machine. Depending on the type of rice and the plant configuration, the industry may include cleaning, dehusking, separation, whitening or polishing, grading and packing.

This page offers a general overview for buyers who want to understand the journey. It should not be read as a technical audit or a claim about the precise process used at The New India Rice Mills Pvt. Ltd.

Six familiar stages in rice milling.

Actual process sequence and machinery can vary by product and mill configuration.

01

Paddy reception

Raw paddy is received and prepared for handling.

02

Cleaning

Unwanted field material and foreign matter are removed.

03

Dehusking

The husk is separated from the rice kernel.

04

Whitening & polishing

Further processing creates the desired finish.

05

Grading

Grains may be separated by size and quality criteria.

06

Packing
