Who we are

Local roots. A practical wholesale focus.

The New India Rice Mills Pvt. Ltd. is located on Arambagh-Kamarpukur Road in Chandur, West Bengal. The business is described as a family-oriented operation specialising in the processing and distribution of essential grains.

Our principal listed rice varieties are Swarna, Ratna, Bashkathi and Minikit. We have structured this website to help buyers understand the range and reach the mill directly to discuss their requirements.
