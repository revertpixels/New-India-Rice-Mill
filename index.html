Welcome to the mill

Rooted in the rice trade. Focused on the people who buy it.

The New India Rice Mills Pvt. Ltd. is a family-oriented wholesale and supply business serving from Chandur, Arambagh, Hooghly. Our work centres on processing and distributing essential grains, with a core focus on boiled rice varieties.

We believe a useful business relationship begins with a clear conversation: which rice variety you need, the quantity you require, and the grade and packaging that suit your order.
